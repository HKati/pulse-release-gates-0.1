# PULSEmech Workshop — Economic Connector Model for AI Monopoly, Human Livelihood, and the Paradoxon Diagram

## Document status

- **Type:** workshop model and mechanical conclusion
- **Language:** English
- **Version:** v0
- **Implementation status:** specification starting point; no active economic or payment system
- **Boundary:** this document does not define legislation, a tax model, or a political program. It records an operational structure.

## 1. Initial theorem

The classical market is not a universal economic law. It is a mechanism that is valid only under defined operating conditions.

A classical market can operate when:

- multiple actors exist independently of one another;
- the actors have access to the production substrate;
- no competitor can unilaterally rewrite the rules of the operating field;
- a producer can retain its own product, customer relation, and revenue path;
- performance can be measured on a stable operating field;
- exclusion can genuinely result from failure of the product, price, quality, or execution.

The monopolistic position of AI removes these preconditions.

The AI layer is not merely a new product within a market. It appears as a general production substrate in writing, programming, design, research, administration, decision preparation, sales, and many other economic processes.

When the same narrow system:

- owns the production substrate;
- controls access;
- determines price and operating conditions;
- observes the usage paths of actors built on top of it;
- enters the dependent layer with its own applications;
- can unilaterally change the substrate, model, or interface,

it is no longer merely a market actor. It is also the owner and rule-maker of the operating field.

Projecting the classical market onto this structure is a **category error**.

## 2. The break in the classical market

The closed loop of the classical money-based market is:

```text
human labor
→ wage income
→ effective demand
→ provider revenue
→ further human labor
```

The production loop of the AI monopoly is:

```text
AI production
→ reduced demand for human labor
→ lower cost and production surplus
→ monopoly or ownership revenue
```

The human can be partially or completely removed from the production side, but does not leave the money-based consumption side. Money remains necessary for food, housing, energy, healthcare, transportation, and social participation.

The break is therefore not merely unemployment:

```text
AI removes the path to wage income
+
the market continues to require money for livelihood
=
a broken human path to economic existence
```

The same system expects this from the human:

```text
pay for your livelihood
```

while doing this:

```text
I no longer purchase the labor
through which you could obtain the money required to pay
```

This is a mechanically open and unsustainable loop.

## 3. Why the classical entrepreneurial response does not solve the break

The conventional response says that the human should:

- retrain;
- perform higher-value work;
- work together with AI;
- start a business;
- undertake creative or specialized tasks.

This is not a general livelihood mechanism.

Not every person can become an entrepreneur with paying customers. A business also requires purchasing power. If human labor income falls at scale, the basis of entrepreneurial revenue—effective demand—falls with it.

Increased productive capability created by AI does not itself create human purchasing power.

The central problem is therefore not which new work AI leaves for the human. The problem is which economic path binds the output of AI production back to human livelihood.

## 4. Why the country is not the correct base unit

The production field of AI does not coincide with state borders.

A single AI system can simultaneously depend on:

- knowledge created in multiple countries;
- multiple linguistic and cultural fields;
- international research;
- data centers operating in different locations;
- users living across the world;
- consumer demand in multiple countries;
- cross-border corporate and financial structures.

The following binding is therefore incorrect:

```text
AI revenue
→ country of the provider's legal seat
→ national tax
→ residents of that country
```

The legal seat is not identical to the production field. The location of a data center is not identical to the source of knowledge. The location of invoicing is not identical to the location of social consequence.

Personal human entitlement must therefore precede territorial accounting.

```text
global AI production event
→ personal human entitlement
→ settlement reconstructible across multiple carriers
→ local currency conversion or direct use
```

A country may act as one carrier for local payment, conversion, identity proof, or dispute handling. It cannot be the source, exclusive owner, or final gateway of the entitlement.

## 5. Why prohibition, taxation, or late redistribution is insufficient

The prohibition-based structure is:

```text
rule
→ violation
→ later investigation
→ possible penalty
```

The operation occurs first. The consequence has already materialized for the human. The provider can treat the penalty as a cost. The rule remains bound to territory, political power, and enforcement capacity.

The taxation structure is:

```text
provider acquires the full revenue
→ state attempts to recover a portion
→ budgetary decision
→ possible human payment
```

This is a late, political, and territorially bound correction.

The mechanical solution does not primarily state what the monopolist may not do. It defines the valid output structure of the economic transition:

```text
AI production value
→ provider output
+
human entitlement output
```

The provider does not first receive the full amount. The human share is not separated later. Both outputs materialize within the same settlement transition.

## 6. The missing link: materialization of the obligation

A declared personal entitlement is not sufficient.

The decisive question is:

> Which mechanism credits the human share before the full AI-produced economic value can become exclusive provider revenue?

The required structure is located in the payment and settlement transition.

The classical transaction is two-party:

```text
payer
→ full amount
→ provider
```

A transaction bound to AI production requires a three-party relation:

```text
payer
├── provider output
└── human entitlement output
```

The three roles are:

1. **Payer or economic value source** — the actor that gives consideration for the AI service, AI-produced product, or machine output.
2. **Provider or production owner** — the actor that operates, develops, or economically uses the AI system.
3. **Human entitlement layer** — the globally personal output that carries the human side of the productive value created by AI.

The money path is not a full amount followed by a later deduction. Settlement creates two final outputs from the beginning.

## 7. The payment protocol alone is still insufficient

A direct API fee, subscription, or license can be identified readily. AI-produced economic value can also appear as:

- value embedded in a product price;
- a renamed hosting or service fee;
- internal corporate cost savings;
- advertising revenue;
- data value;
- payroll displaced through automation;
- invoicing through another company;
- part of a physical or digital product produced with AI.

The invoice label is therefore not the subject to be observed. The AI-materialized economic event must be identified.

```text
use of AI capability
→ economic output
→ revenue, savings, or production surplus
→ change in the human income path
→ settlement event
```

The payment protocol only executes the output of an already identified and verified event. It does not decide the size of the human share.

## 8. The role of the Paradoxon Diagram

The missing measuring and connecting layer is not a new central development environment.

It is the economic application of the original PULSE Paradoxon Diagram.

The Paradoxon Diagram runs in parallel with online operation. It is not an authority, distributor, payment provider, or direct intervention mechanism.

Its task is to:

- identify both sides of the same AI production event;
- measure the relation between machine production surplus and reduction of the human income path;
- reconnect consequences that the linear economic system treats separately;
- create a reproducible bound state imprint for the verifier and materializer.

### 8.1. The economic paradox

The AI provider's interest is:

```text
greater machine production capability
→ lower human labor requirement
→ greater production surplus and revenue
```

The other side of the same transition is:

```text
lower human labor requirement
→ lower labor income
→ lower effective demand
→ lower realizable provider revenue
```

By displacing labor, the provider simultaneously increases productive capability and dismantles its own field of effective demand.

These are not two separate social problems. They are two connected outputs of the same AI production event.

### 8.2. The two sides of the diagram

**Machine production side:**

```text
prior human labor requirement
→ new AI-operated production state
→ reduced human labor requirement
→ cost savings
→ production surplus
→ provider economic output
```

**Human income-path side:**

```text
prior employment relation
→ prior labor income
→ AI displacement
→ reduced path to money
→ reduced purchasing power
→ livelihood gap
```

**The relation:**

```text
productive advantage captured through AI
↔
financial gap created on the human side
```

The Paradoxon Diagram does not select an arbitrary percentage. It emits the measured relation established between the two sides.

## 10. Separation of roles

The system avoids creating a new monopoly only if the roles do not collapse into one another.

### Paradoxon Diagram

Measures and connects. It does not decide, pay, or store final entitlement.

### Verifier

Reproduces or rejects the measured relation state. Its own position is not evidence.

### Settlement-share materializer

Creates the two entitlement outputs from the verified state. It does not arbitrarily modify the measurement.

### Payment protocol

Executes the materialized outputs. It does not determine the share or rewrite the entitlement.

### Settlement carriers

Record and transmit personal entitlement. A carrier is not the source of entitlement.

### Local financial system

Converts, pays, or provides direct use. It neither creates nor deletes the global human entitlement.

## 11. Central invariants of the PULSE pattern

### 11.1. Position is not evidence

```text
provider ownership
≠
settlement-share authority
```

```text
state jurisdiction
≠
source of personal global entitlement
```

```text
settlement provider
≠
owner of the entitlement
```

### 11.2. The carrier is not the operating state

A bank, ledger, digital account, or local currency can be lost, replaced, or blocked. Personal entitlement remains reconstructible from multiple independent carriers.

```text
carrier ≠ entitlement ≠ authority
```

### 11.3. A provider assertion does not materialize an output

The provider may supply data and evidence. Its assertion alone cannot create final human or provider settlement.

### 11.4. No unilateral finalization

No company, country, international organization, verifier, or settlement operator can create, modify, or delete a final personal entitlement state solely by invoking its position.

### 11.5. AI revenue is incomplete when the human output is missing

```text
human_output = missing
→ settlement_state = incomplete
→ provider_revenue_state = non-final
```

This is not a later classification of a violation. It is an incomplete economic transition state.

### 11.6. Reproducibility

The same event, input binding, policy, and verifier conditions must produce the same output.

## 12. Structure of the human output

Human entitlement is not aid and is not a discretionary grant from the provider.

Within the model, the same AI production event creates:

- provider economic advantage;
- displacement of human labor;
- reduction of the human income path;
- feedback into demand.

The human output is therefore part of the original settlement structure of the event.

The personal output can be divided into at least two layers:

```text
universal human base entitlement
+
additional entitlement bound to proven direct relations
```

The universal layer is bound to every human because AI produces from a general human field of knowledge, language, culture, and economic activity.

The relation-specific layer may be bound, for example, to:

- a directly displaced employment relation;
- a provable creator or data-source relation;
- an infrastructure or energy contribution;
- a direct social consequence.

These layers cannot override the universal personal base.

## 13. The settlement unit

In this model, the personal PULSE unit is not new speculative money and is not an independent cryptocurrency.

It represents a verified claim:

> a personal partial entitlement to the human output of already-created and verified AI economic value.

```text
verified AI economic value
→ human settlement-share state
→ personal PULSE settlement unit
→ local currency conversion or direct use
```

A PULSE unit is not created without backing. It is bound to a verified AI economic event.

## 14. Resolving the power problem between countries

The power problem is not resolved by every country signing the same agreement. That would remain an arrangement determined by power relations and enforcement bargaining between countries.

The problem is resolved when the country is not authority over creation of the entitlement.

```text
global AI production event
→ verified personal entitlement
→ territory-independent settlement
→ local conversion
```

A country cannot decide:

- whether the entitlement exists;
- whether the entitlement of a person living in another country can be deleted;
- whether the global human share is its own budgetary property;
- whether creation of personal entitlement depends on a political condition.

The local system is an execution and conversion node. It is not a final gateway.

The entitlement is multi-channel and survives country change. Failure of one local bank or state channel does not delete the state.

## 15. What makes the system unavoidable?

Not a single global payment gateway. That would create a new settlement monopoly.

Unavoidability is produced by multiple independent proof and settlement bindings:

```text
AI service identity
+
compute and runtime provenance
+
economic-event binding
+
Paradoxon Diagram measurement
+
independent verification
+
dual output
+
payment settlement
+
accounting and audit finality
→ final AI economic revenue state
```

A provider may technically accept money through a bypass. That amount does not receive the same verified, auditable, insurable, and final AI-revenue state.

The system derives its force from the conditions of finality, not from prohibition.

## 16. Implementation boundary

The mechanical structure of the concept can be recorded. The following elements require separate specifications and proofs:

1. **Identification of an AI production event** — for direct and product-embedded AI use.
2. **Binding of the prior human-work relation** — by reconstructing the actual preceding state.
3. **Measurement of economic value** — distinguishing revenue, cost savings, and production surplus.
4. **Measurement of income-path loss** — by modeling the actual relation between human income and purchasing power.
5. **Paradoxon Diagram policy** — with versioned, subject-bound, and reproducible measurement rules.
6. **Independent verifier network** — without final authority residing in a single verifier.
7. **Proof of personal entitlement** — without exclusive country-dependent or provider-dependent identity.
8. **Settlement reconstructible across multiple carriers** — without a single ledger or key owner.
9. **Local redemption** — with proof of the relation between global entitlement and local money.
10. **Correction and replay** — for incorrect measurement, late evidence, or disputed events.
11. **Privacy** — without appropriation of the data needed to measure labor displacement and economic output.
12. **Closure of bypass paths** — for renamed fees, internal accounting, intermediary companies, and non-monetary consideration.

These are engineering tasks. They do not override the central structure.

## 18. Final conclusion

The failure of the classical market under an AI monopoly is not merely the absence of competition.

The classical market binds the monetary path to livelihood to human labor. The AI monopoly removes that labor path while preserving the money-based requirement for livelihood.

The break cannot be solved by searching for new work for the human inside the same structure. It cannot be solved by late taxation, territorial redistribution, or prohibition-based rules.

The complete economic transition must materialize two inseparable outputs:

```text
AI production event
→ provider economic output
+
personal human entitlement output
```

The Paradoxon Diagram is the connecting layer. Within the same AI production event, it measures the relation between machine production surplus and loss of the human income path. The verifier reproduces the measurement. The materializer creates the dual entitlement state. The payment protocol executes it. Personal entitlement precedes the country and local financial conversion.

The central theorem is:

> **AI-produced value cannot become complete, final, and exclusively provider-owned revenue unless the bound personal human entitlement output has materialized within the same transition.**

The economic machine closes as follows:

```text
human society
→ AI production substrate
→ machine-produced value
→ dual settlement output
→ human livelihood access
→ preserved purchasing power
→ effective demand
→ further production
```

The Paradoxon Diagram did not appear as a new system. In this model, it found the global economic connector role for which its parallel measuring mechanism is suited.
